Welcome to the Theatre

The Grand Royale Theatre has stood on the corner of King Street for over a century. Behind its red-brick facade you will find a 450-seat auditorium, a horseshoe balcony and one of the finest Victorian stages still in daily use. Every year we stage a mix of classic drama, new writing, family pantomime and live music, and we run workshops for local schools. Whether you are visiting for the first time or you have had the same seat for twenty years, we look forward to welcoming you.

Tickets can be booked at the box office, by telephone, or online. Concessions are available for students, seniors and groups of ten or more. Take a look at our current program to see what is on.
